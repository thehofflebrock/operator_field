## Rule changes worth noting

- **Absence.** The first analyst rules said to report silence as a finding outright. That produced narratives about who went quiet. The later rule: absence counts only against an established baseline of presence.
- **Tone.** Daily v1 banned any note on tone. v3 reversed it: the poster's own stance is signal; the model's opinion of the post is contamination.
- **Filtering.** The Chain filtered at capture. Every later version filters at read. The June 10 comparison is why.
