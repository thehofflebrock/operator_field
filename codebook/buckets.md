# Bucket Codebook

TYPE: CODEBOOK
Status: derived from the 12-account AI roster, June 2026. Retired when the roster expanded across domains; a new derivation week replaced it.

## Minting rule

A bucket lives by multi-author recurrence across days, or by being single-author with a multi-day subject no other bucket can house (the housed-orphan clause). A bucket that collapses when its loudest author is removed is that author's channel, not a field topic.

## Noise floor

The only filter. Drops posts with no subject of their own. No domain test.

## Buckets

**PRODUCT** (provisional)
Launches, features, and model releases. Held pending multi-author confirmation.

**CAPITAL**
Company money and markets: valuations, IPOs, burn rate, fund and board moves, enterprise ROI, business-model viability, hiring read as strategy.
Not COMPUTE. CAPITAL owns financing and returns.

**COMPUTE**
Hardware and the buildout. The iron, not the money. Minted on multi-author recurrence across days.

**LABOR**
What AI does to work and output: jobs, labor markets, productivity, macro consequences. Not firm finances.
Kept under the housed-orphan clause; flagged fragile because authorship is thin.

**GOVERNANCE**
Policy, regulation, legal and institutional action bearing on AI and tech.
General politics, crime, culture, and demographics go to residual.

**PHILOSOPHY**
What AI or intelligence is: AGI definitions, machine consciousness, AI's role in knowledge.
Run strictly. A metaphysical claim enters only if it bears on AI or machine intelligence.

**PERSONAL / META**
The person on the platform rather than the field: personal life, milestones, self-promotion outside their work, platform meta.
The line is subject, not tone. An informal post about compute is COMPUTE.

## Residual

A post that cleared the noise floor and fits no bucket. Residual is the mint-candidate signal.

Watched clusters, deliberately unminted:
- **POLITICS / SOCIETY** and **SPACE**: mostly one high-volume account. Tested by removing it.
- **SCIENCE / RESEARCH**: single-author. Mints if it spreads across authors; otherwise it stays that author's channel.
